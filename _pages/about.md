Hello! I am an incoming Associate Data Analyst on the Contract Administration & Performance team at National Grid. I have a passion for data analytics, statistical storytelling, and building robust and user-friendly data systems. I recently completed my degree in Statistics and Computer Science at Boston University, where I enjoyed working with data in Python, SQL, and R, alongside thinking about lower-level systems concepts and implementation decisions. I couple this data science experience with my strong background in the humanities and my work as a teaching assistant for both introductory and advanced computer science courses. Both of these factors make me an effective and empathetic communicator for expert and non-expert stakeholders and a more holistic data analyst.

I am a proud alumnus of Boston University's [Core Curriculum](https://www.bu.edu/core/about/welcome/) program, an elective, interdisciplinary, writing-intensive exploration of enduring works in the humanities and social sciences. Alongside this program, I also completed a minor in Spanish. In my free time, I enjoy practicing my Spanish, reading, and hiking.

For a sample of my projects, please see my [Github](https://github.com/clarkaid). If you would like to contact me, please message me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aidanclark42/).
